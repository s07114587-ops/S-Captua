Privacy Policy

S-Captcha values user privacy. Our security infrastructure strictly adheres to privacy-first guidelines and zero-data-logging principles.

1. Data Encryption (SHA-256)

We do not collect or store plain IP addresses or personal browsing data. Any IP information processed during verification is immediately hashed using SHA-256 cryptography for anonymized bot mitigation.

2. No Activity Tracking & History Auto-Purge

S-Captcha never tracks personal browsing history across websites. All verification logs automatically expire and get cleared every 12 hours.
